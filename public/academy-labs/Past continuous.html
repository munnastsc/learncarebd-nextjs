Ultimate Modern 3D Master Sheet: Past Continuous Tense (Extended)

1. Short Trick & Core Rules

Core Structure: Subject + was/were + Main Verb(ing) + Extension

Golden Rule: **I was doing** = আমি করছিলাম। (Use 'was' for I/He/She/It, and 'were' for We/You/They).

10 Sentences Changing Subjects:

  • I was playing cricket. - আমি ক্রিকেট খেলছিলাম।
  • We were reading books. - আমরা বই পড়ছিলাম।
  • You were writing a letter. - তুমি একটি চিঠি লিখছিলে।
  • They were going to school. - তারা স্কুলে যাচ্ছিল।
  • He was eating rice. - সে ভাত খাচ্ছিল।
  • She was singing a song. - সে গান গাইছিল।
  • Rahim was speaking English. - রহিম ইংরেজি বলছিল।
  • It was raining yesterday. - গতকাল বৃষ্টি হচ্ছিল।
  • Birds were flying in the sky. - পাখিরা আকাশে উড়ছিল।
  • Children were eating sweets. - শিশুরা মিষ্টি খাচ্ছিল।

The 4-Way Sentence Framework:

Form Structure Example Bangla Meaning
Affirmative Subject + was/were + V(ing) + Obj I was doing আমি করছিলাম
Negative Subject + was/were + not + V(ing) I was not doing আমি করছিলাম না
Interrogative Was/Were + Subject + V(ing)? Was I doing? আমি কি করছিলাম?
Negative Interrogative Was/Were + Subject + not + V(ing)?
Or: Wasn't/Weren't + Sub + V(ing)?		 Wasn't I doing? আমি কি করছিলাম না?
