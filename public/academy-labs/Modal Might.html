📘 1. Short Trick & Core Rules

১. Modal 'Might' এর প্রধান ব্যবহারসমূহ

১. Weak Possibility (ক্ষীণ বা কম সম্ভাবনা - ২০-৩০%)

কোনো ঘটনা ঘটার সম্ভাবনা যখন খুবই কম বা অনিশ্চিত, তখন May এর বদলে Might বসে।
তুলনা: It may rain (৫০% সম্ভাবনা) বনাম It might rain (আকাশ পরিষ্কার, তবে বৃষ্টি হতেও পারে - খুব কম সম্ভাবনা)।

২. Past Form of 'May' (Indirect Speech এ)

Direct Speech এ 'may' থাকলে Indirect Speech এ তা পরিবর্তিত হয়ে might হয়।
উদাহরণ: He said, "I may come" ➔ He said that he might come.

৩. Tentative / Polite Suggestions (বিনম্র দ্বিধাদ্বন্দ্বযুক্ত পরামর্শ)

খুবই নরম ও দ্বিধাগ্রস্ত সুরে কাউকে কোনো পরামর্শ দিতে 'might' বসে।
উদাহরণ: You might want to check the train schedule once again.

৪. Past Hypothetical: 'Might have + V3' (হতে পারত কিন্তু হয়নি)

অতীতের কোনো সম্ভাবনা যা ঘটতে পারত কিন্তু শেষ পর্যন্ত ঘটেনি।
উদাহরণ: You might have slipped on the wet floor! (তুমি পিছলে পড়ে যেতে পারতে!)

২. The 6 Golden Spoken Sentence Structures

Pattern No.Structure NameFormulaPractical Spoken Example
1Affirmative (+)Sub + might + V1 + ObjectHe might arrive a bit late tonight.
2Negative (-)Sub + might not + V1 + ObjectThey might not like this bitter coffee.
3Interrogative (?)Might + Sub + V1 + Object...?Might I suggest a better alternative?
4Past Speculation (Past)Sub + might have + V3 + ObjectShe might have forgotten our appointment.
5Tentative SuggestionSub + might want to + V1...You might want to take a sweater; it's chilly.
6Conditional (2nd)If + Sub + V2, Sub + might + V1If he tried hard, he might win the race.

1. What degree of possibility does 'Might' usually express compared to 'May'?

A) Weaker possibility (20-30%)
B) Stronger possibility (90%)
C) Absolute guarantee (100%)
✓ Correct Answer: A — 'Might' expresses a remote, weak, or hesitant possibility in spoken English.

2. What is the past form of 'May' in indirect speech?

A) might
B) could
C) would
✓ Correct Answer: A — Direct: 'may' ➔ Indirect: 'might' (He said he might come).

3. Which sentence expresses a past possibility that didn't happen?

A) You might have hurt your leg!
B) You might hurt your leg.
C) You may hurt your leg.
✓ Correct Answer: A — 'Might have + V3' expresses past speculation or hypothetical danger.

4. Choose the polite tentative suggestion:

A) You might want to review this draft.
B) You must review this draft.
C) You will review this draft.
✓ Correct Answer: A — 'You might want to...' is a courteous, soft, non-demanding suggestion.

5. Fill in: 'I'm not sure, but Salman ___ know the answer.'

A) might
B) must to
C) should to
✓ Correct Answer: A — 'Might know' expresses modest possibility with uncertainty.

6. Complete the sentence: 'She might have ___ her car keys at home.'

A) forgotten
B) forget
C) forgetting
✓ Correct Answer: A — After 'might have', always use the past participle (V3) form: 'forgotten'.

7. What tone does 'Might I suggest...' convey?

A) Extremely polite, diplomatic, and humble
B) Angry and demanding
C) Casual and slang
✓ Correct Answer: A — 'Might I suggest...' is very formal, deferential, and polite.

8. Select the correct negative form:

A) He might not come to class today.
B) He does not might come.
C) He might not comes.
✓ Correct Answer: A — Negative structure: Subject + might not + Base Verb.

9. Which sentence is used in the 2nd Conditional?

A) If he worked harder, he might pass the exam.
B) If he works, he might pass.
C) If he will work, he might pass.
✓ Correct Answer: A — 2nd Conditional: If + Past Simple (worked), Subject + might + Base Verb (pass).

10. Fill in: 'Look at the sky; it's mostly clear, but it ___ rain later.'

A) might
B) must
C) shall
✓ Correct Answer: A — Clear sky with slight chance uses 'might' for remote possibility.

11. Identify the correct question structure:

A) Might I ask a personal question?
B) Do I might ask a question?
C) Might I asking a question?
✓ Correct Answer: A — Interrogative begins with 'Might + Subject + V1...?'

12. What does 'might have missed' imply in: 'He might have missed the bus'?

A) Speculation about why he hasn't arrived in the past
B) He definitely caught the bus
C) He will catch the bus tomorrow
✓ Correct Answer: A — It speculates on a probable past cause for a current situation.

13. Choose the correct translation: 'সে হয়তো আজকে আসতে পারে (সম্ভাবনা কম)।'

A) He might come today.
B) He must come today.
C) He should come today.
✓ Correct Answer: A — Weak possibility is expressed by 'might come'.

14. Fill in: 'You ___ want to carry an umbrella just in case.'

A) might
B) ought
C) dared
✓ Correct Answer: A — 'You might want to...' is the standard spoken idiom for gentle advice.

15. Select the error in: 'She might can solve the puzzle.'

A) Two modal verbs cannot be used together; use 'might be able to'
B) It should be 'might could'
C) No error
✓ Correct Answer: A — Double modals are ungrammatical; replace 'can' with 'be able to'.

16. Complete: 'Why didn't you inform us? We ___ have helped you!'

A) might
B) must to
C) ought
✓ Correct Answer: A — 'We might have helped you' expresses unfulfilled past possibility.

17. Choose the sentence with proper verb agreement:

A) They might arrive before sunset.
B) They might arrives before sunset.
C) They might arrived before sunset.
✓ Correct Answer: A — Modals always take the bare base infinitive 'arrive' without -s or -ed.

18. Fill in: 'Be careful with that knife; you ___ cut your finger!'

A) might
B) shall
C) ought
✓ Correct Answer: A — 'might cut' warns of an unintended potential accident.

19. What does 'might not' express in: 'The shop might not be open yet'?

A) Uncertainty about current open status
B) A strict ban on opening
C) Inability to open
✓ Correct Answer: A — It expresses tentative doubt or uncertainty.

20. Select the correct spoken response: 'Where did Amina go?'

A) 'She might have gone to the grocery store.'
B) 'She might went to the store.'
C) 'She might going to the store.'
✓ Correct Answer: A — 'might have gone' (Modal + have + V3) correctly speculates on past action.
