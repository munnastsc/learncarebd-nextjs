📘 1. Short Trick & Core Rules

১. চেনার সহজ কৌশল ও মূল কনসেপ্ট (Past of the Past)

অতীতকালে দুটি কাজ সংঘটিত হয়েছিল—তার মধ্যে যে কাজটি অপেক্ষাকৃত আগে ঘটেছিল, সেটি হবে Past Perfect Tense। আর যে কাজটি পরে ঘটেছিল, সেটি হবে Past Simple (Indefinite) Tense।

Golden Spoken Rule:
• Past Perfect: Subject + had + Verb3 (Past Participle) + Object
• Past Simple: Subject + Verb2 (Past Form) + Object

'Before' এর ম্যাজিক নিয়ম

Past Perfect + BEFORE + Past Simple
অর্থাৎ, Before এর আগে Had + V3 বসে।
উদাহরণ: The train had left before we reached the station.

'After' এর ম্যাজিক নিয়ম

Past Simple + AFTER + Past Perfect
অর্থাৎ, After এর পরে Had + V3 বসে।
উদাহরণ: The doctor came after the patient had died.

২. The 6 Golden Spoken Sentence Structures

Pattern No.Structure NameFormulaPractical Spoken Example
1Affirmative (+)Sub + had + V3 + Obj + before/after...I had finished my dinner before 9 PM.
2Negative (-)Sub + had + NOT + V3 + Obj...She had not locked the door before leaving.
3Interrogative (?)Had + Sub + V3 + Obj...?Had you informed the teacher before the exam?
4Negative Interrogative (-?)Hadn't + Sub + V3 + Obj...?Hadn't they reached home before the storm hit?
5WH-Question (+)WH + had + Sub + V3 + Obj...?What had you discussed before I arrived?
6WH-Question (-)WH + hadn't + Sub + V3 + Obj...?Why hadn't you submitted the paper on time?
